Speaker Connect: Volume Boost

Transform your iPhone into an all-in-one Bluetooth & AirPlay speaker routing hub, 10-band graphic equalizer with dual Bass & Volume Boosters, live microphone megaphone with pitch shifting, and video-to-audio extractor.

Key Features

10-Band Studio EQ & Dual Boosters

Fine-tune audio across 10 frequency bands (31Hz–16kHz, -12dB to +12dB), dial in 0–100% Bass Booster and Volume Booster gains, and switch between 9 acoustic presets or your own custom curves.

Instant Bluetooth & AirPlay Routing

Pair and switch between wireless Bluetooth 5.3 and AirPlay 2 speakers and headphones in one tap with real-time connection status, signal telemetry, and optional Bluetooth autoplay.

Live Microphone, Megaphone & Recorder

Broadcast your voice live from your iPhone to any connected speaker with real-time pitch shifting (-12 to +12 semitones), mic gain boost, neon spectrum visualization, and HD voice recording.

Video-to-Audio Extractor & Smart Alarm

Extract audio tracks from Camera Roll videos, import MP3/WAV/FLAC/M4A files, control 0.5x–2.0x playback speed, set custom Music Alarms and fade-out Sleep Timers, and trigger Siri Shortcuts.
