C. DATA SETS WE COLLECT AND FOR WHAT PURPOSE:

Speaker Connect: Volume Boost is built with a strict zero-server, local-first, 100% on-device audio processing architecture. We do not require you to register an account or log in. All 10-band equalizer DSP filtering, Bass & Volume Booster amplification, Bluetooth and AirPlay route discovery, live microphone voice broadcasting, pitch shifting, voice recording, and Camera Roll video-to-audio extraction run locally on your iOS device using Apple’s native AVAudioEngine, AVFoundation, CoreBluetooth, and MediaPlayer frameworks. We do not operate backend servers that collect, upload, or store your personal audio files, voice recordings, playlists, or browsing history.

Non-Personal Data

During your interaction with the App, we process non-identifiable studio preference and configuration settings locally on your device (“Non-Personal Data”), such as your selected interface language (across 19 supported locales), 10-band equalizer gain curves, custom EQ presets, Bass/Volume Booster levels, playback speed (0.5x–2.0x), repeat mode, haptic feedback toggle, audio fade-out preference, Bluetooth autoplay toggle, and Sleep Timer countdown configuration.

We do not knowingly collect or process any Special Categories of Personal Data (such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, genetic data, biometric voiceprint identification, or health data).
